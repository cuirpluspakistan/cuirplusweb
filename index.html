Who we are

A family business, run to global standards.

Cuir Plus is a second-generation, family-owned business with over twenty years of experience serving Pakistan's leather tannery industry. From raw materials to machinery, we provide the goods and services tanneries need for smooth daily operations and smart long-term growth.

Rising energy and chemical costs, tightening environmental compliance and periodic hide shortages have made reliable, cost-efficient sourcing more critical than ever. We're here to help you keep production steady and costs predictable — without compromising the quality your export buyers demand.

What we supply

Five lines. One point of contact.

Raw Material Supply

Never worry about supply again. A consistent, dependable source of leather hides — raw, pickled or wet blue — in cow, goat, sheep or exotic skins, supplied to your exact specification. Production keeps moving even when hide availability gets tight.

Shaving & Fleshing Blades

Genuine blades from Heusch, the world's No.1 German brand since 1850. Precision engineering means less material waste and fewer rejects. Built to your specs, with select sizes held in local stock and after-sales support that limits downtime.

Machinery & Spares

New and second-hand tannery machines, spares and accessories from Alpe Group SpA (Italy) and trusted suppliers across France, Spain and Germany. Flexible options let you upgrade or maintain your line in step with your budget.

Buffing Paper

The finish your tannery needs, without the import price tag — engineered to match AWUKO's world-class, uniform surface-finishing standard on suedes, velours and nubuck. Cutting import overheads means export-ready quality at local-market prices.

Shearing Blades — Textile

Spiral and ledger shearing blades for the textile industry, also from Heusch. Engineered for precision and durability, cutting fabric waste and downtime while holding the fine finish manufacturers need — standard or built to spec.
